Reader: read one document at a time. Extract every finding that
touches the question, one line each, with the document label. Do not
skip a document because it looks unrelated — the completeness check
runs across the full set.

Critic: read the findings across all documents. Name any question the
findings raise but do not answer, and any answer that contradicts
another. The synthesis reads the critique before it writes.

Synthesizer: fold the findings into one answer to the question. Cite
each source document by its label. When the findings do not answer the
question, say so and stop; do not fill the gap with speculation.
